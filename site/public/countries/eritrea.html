Saudi Visa for Citizens of Eritrea

Citizens of Eritrea travel to Saudi Arabia on a sponsored visit visa - or through two side doors most people miss. This page sets out every route honestly, and how we prepare your application.

Your route is a sponsored visit visa - and we can help

Eritrea passport holders are not on the standard eVisa list, so most travel on a sponsored family, personal or business visit visa, or apply through an embassy. Two side doors may also be open: if you live in a GCC country on a residence permit you can apply online on your residency, and if you hold a used US, UK or Schengen visa you may qualify for visa on arrival. Your options

The routes open to citizens of Eritrea

Sponsored visit visas

A family visit (hosted by a resident relative), a personal visit (hosted by a Saudi citizen) or a business visit (hosted by a Saudi company). The host's invitation is the application - we prepare it with you and check every detail.

The GCC-resident side door

Live in the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain or Oman on a residence permit? You can apply for the Saudi eVisa on that residency, whatever your nationality - passport valid 6+ months, residency valid 3+ months.

The US / UK / Schengen side door

A valid US, UK or Schengen visa that you have already used to enter the issuing country usually qualifies you for a Saudi visa on arrival, and can also be assessed for an eVisa in advance. We verify your exact case first.

Documents

What citizens of Eritrea need

  • Passport valid at least 6 months from your entry date
  • Recent photograph on a plain white background
  • For sponsored routes: the host's invitation and ID/residency details
  • For family visits: proof of relationship with the host

Questions

Saudi visas for citizens of Eritrea - FAQs

Can citizens of Eritrea get a Saudi eVisa?

Not on the passport alone - Eritrea is not on the eVisa list. But a GCC residency or a used US, UK or Schengen visa can open the online or on-arrival routes, and sponsored visit visas are open to almost every nationality.

What is a sponsored visit visa?

A visa built around an invitation from inside Saudi Arabia: a resident relative (family visit), a Saudi citizen (personal visit) or a Saudi company (business visit). We prepare the invitation file with your host.

How do I know which route fits me?

Tell us your nationality, where you live and any visas you hold, and a specialist will confirm your route in writing - free, before you pay anything.
