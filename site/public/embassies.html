Embassies

Saudi embassies & consulates worldwide

Find the official Saudi mission for your country. One thing first, because it matters: the embassies are the Saudi government's - we are a private visa service in Dubai. This page simply points you to the right official door.

When do you actually need an embassy?

Most travellers never do - the eVisa, visa on arrival and sponsored routes cover the great majority of journeys, and you can check your route in under a minute. A Saudi mission is the right door for these:

Embassy-route applications

Some visa types and nationalities are handled in person by a Saudi embassy or consulate. If your route says “embassy”, the mission for your country of residence is where that application belongs.

Document attestation

Saudi missions attest documents - degrees, marriage and birth certificates, commercial papers - for use inside the Kingdom. Attestation is the mission's own service, arranged with them directly.

Saudi citizens abroad

For Saudi citizens living or travelling abroad, the embassy is their government's front desk: consular help, emergencies and passport services.

Find your country's Saudi mission

Click your country on the map - or search for it - to open its page: your visa route, the documents you need, and the official Saudi mission website for your country, all in one place. 105 countries have their own mission website on the Saudi government's domains (saudiembassy.sa); for the rest, the official embassy directory shows which mission covers you.

Tip: on a phone, the search is the fastest way to your country - or tap a region to zoom the map in.
