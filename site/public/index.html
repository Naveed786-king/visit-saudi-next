Before you apply

The Saudi tourist eVisa at a glance

Who can apply

Passport holders of 72 eligible countries apply online. GCC residents apply on their residence permit, US, UK and Schengen visa holders have their own routes, and everyone else travels on a sponsored visit visa. See the full country list.

Validity & stay

Valid one year from issue, multiple entry, up to 90 days per visit. Fourteen nationalities receive a single-entry 30-day version instead - we confirm which applies to you before you apply.

What it costs

The official fee includes the mandatory medical insurance. Our service fee is added clearly on top and your total is confirmed in writing before any payment - no hidden charges, ever.

Processing time

Most straightforward eVisas are decided within a few working days. Track every stage with your reference, from document check to eVisa ready.

Umrah on a tourist eVisa

Umrah is allowed on the tourist eVisa outside the Hajj season, with a Nusuk permit for your slots. Hajj always needs its own visa through official channels.

What you need

A passport valid at least six months, a recent photo on a white background and a few minutes online. No hotel booking or flight ticket is needed for the standard eVisa application.
